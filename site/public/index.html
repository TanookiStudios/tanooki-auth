What we promise, in writing

No account
There is nothing to sign up for, so there is no password of ours to leak and no profile of you to sell.
Nothing leaves your device
Your codes live in one file on your own device, locked with AES-256. The key sits in your device’s secure storage.
No ads, no analytics
The app holds no tracking code. We never see which sites you use or how often.
Works offline
Codes come from your device’s clock, so they keep working on a plane or with Wi‑Fi off.

From QR code to signed in

  1. Scan it

    Point your camera at the QR code, or open a screenshot of it. Leaving Google Authenticator? One scan brings every account over.

  2. Fill it in

    Tap a code to copy it. On your computer, the browser extension fills it in for you, only on the site it belongs to, talking only to the app on that computer.

  3. Back it up

    Save an encrypted backup with a password only you know, then open it on your next phone or computer.
