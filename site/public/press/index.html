About Tanooki Auth

Tanooki Auth is a free two-factor authenticator for Mac, Windows and Android, with iPhone and iPad on the way to the App Store. There is no account to make and no cloud: your codes live in one file on your own device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM, and they keep working offline. You can scan a QR code or a screenshot of one, bring every account over from Google Authenticator in one scan, and save an encrypted backup with a password only you know. A browser extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge fills codes in, only on the site each one belongs to, and talks only to the app on the same computer. The app has no ads and no analytics, and its source code is public under the GPL-3.0 license. An optional one-time $5 Premium adds more themes and app icons, end-to-end encrypted sync, approving codes from your phone, breach alerts and secure account sharing. Tanooki Auth is made by Tanooki Studios.

Features

Free, on every platform

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for.
  • Codes stay on your device, in one file encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is kept in the system’s secure storage (Keychain, DPAPI or Android Keystore).
  • Works offline. Standard time-based and counter-based codes (RFC 6238 and RFC 4226).
  • Unlimited accounts. Add them by scanning a QR code or opening a screenshot of one.
  • Moves every account over from Google Authenticator in one scan.
  • Browser extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge. It pairs once with a 6-digit code, talks only to the app on the same computer, and fills a code only on the exact site it is saved for.
  • Encrypted backups with a password only you know (PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600,000 rounds, then AES-256-GCM).
  • An app lock.
  • No ads and no analytics.
  • Open source under the GPL-3.0 license.

Premium, $5 once

  • More themes, including Pastel Pink and Pastel Blue, and different app icons.
  • Sync across your devices, end-to-end encrypted: through iCloud on Apple devices, or the Tanooki relay for any mix.
  • Approve codes from your phone when the browser extension asks.
  • Breach alerts when a site you have an account for is in a known data breach, checked on your device.
  • Share one account with someone you trust using a QR code and a 6-digit code. It is locked and expires in 24 hours.

Platforms

  • Mac (macOS 12 or newer), Windows (Windows 10 or newer), Android (Android 7 or newer).
  • iPhone and iPad are coming to the App Store.
