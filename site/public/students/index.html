Students get Premium for half price

Tanooki Auth is free. Premium (sync, themes and more) is a one-time $5, and students pay $2.50.

How it works

Use the email address your school or university gave you, such as one ending in .edu, .ac.uk or .edu.au. We email you a link that takes you to checkout with the discount already applied. The link works once and lasts 7 days.

We don't keep your email address. If your school's address isn't recognized, get in touch through tanookistudios.com/support and we'll add it.
