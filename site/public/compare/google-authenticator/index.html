Tanooki Auth vs Google Authenticator

Google Authenticator, made by Google, is a two-factor authenticator app. Here is how the two compare on facts you can check yourself.

What Google Authenticator does well

  • If you sign in, your codes sync to your Google Account, so a lost phone doesn’t mean lost codes. Source
  • Moving to a new phone is simple: the old phone shows QR codes and the new one scans them, with or without a Google Account. Source

Moving from Google Authenticator to Tanooki Auth

Tanooki Auth reads Google Authenticator’s transfer QR codes. In Google Authenticator, open the menu, then Transfer accounts, then Export accounts, and in Tanooki Auth tap Add account, then Move from Google Authenticator, and scan each QR code it shows.

For step-by-step help on a particular website, see the two-factor guides.
