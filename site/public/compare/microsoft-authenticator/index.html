What Microsoft Authenticator does well

  • Passwordless sign-in for Microsoft accounts: type your username, then approve with your face, fingerprint or PIN. Source
  • Push approvals: a sign-in request pops up on your phone and you tap to approve it. Source

Moving from Microsoft Authenticator to Tanooki Auth

We couldn’t find an export option in Microsoft’s documentation. The safe way to move is one account at a time: on each website, turn two-factor off and back on (or add a new authenticator app), and scan the new QR code with Tanooki Auth. Keep Microsoft Authenticator until every account works.

For step-by-step help on a particular website, see the two-factor guides.
