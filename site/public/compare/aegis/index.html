What Aegis does well

  • An encrypted vault you unlock with a password or fingerprint, and the app never goes online. Source
  • Imports from many other apps, including Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator and 2FAS. Source

Moving from Aegis to Tanooki Auth

Tanooki Auth opens Aegis export files, encrypted or not. In Aegis, open Settings, then Import & Export, and export as Aegis (.JSON). Then in Tanooki Auth tap Add account, then Move from another app, and pick that file.
