What Authy does well

  • Keeps your codes in sync across several phones and tablets, and lets you remove a lost device. Source
  • Its cloud backup is encrypted with your own password before it leaves your phone. Source

Moving from Authy to Tanooki Auth

We couldn’t find an export option in Authy’s documentation. The safe way to move is one account at a time: on each website, turn two-factor off and back on, and scan the new QR code with Tanooki Auth. Keep Authy until every account works.

For step-by-step help on a particular website, see the two-factor guides.
