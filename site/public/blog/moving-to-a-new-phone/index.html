Moving to a new phone without losing your codes

November 2, 2026

New phone day is exciting. The one way to ruin it is wiping the old phone before your codes have moved. Here’s the safe order.

1. Don’t wipe or trade in the old phone yet

Keep it working until you’ve confirmed every account on the new one. Microsoft gives the same advice: some accounts have to be signed into again on the new phone, so hold on to the old one until that’s done.

2. Move your codes

How depends on your app:

  • Tanooki Auth: make a fresh encrypted backup on the old device and open it on the new one, or turn on Premium sync. You can also show an account as a QR code and scan it on the new phone.
  • Google Authenticator: Google explains that synced codes appear when you sign in on the new phone; otherwise, use Transfer accounts to show QR codes and scan them.
  • Microsoft Authenticator: Microsoft notes you can only restore to the same kind of phone, iPhone to iPhone or Android to Android.

Switching apps at the same time? Tanooki Auth can bring accounts over from Google Authenticator, Aegis and 2FAS. Our comparison pages explain how for each app.

3. Test every account

Open each account in the new app and check that its code works: sign in to the site, or compare the code with the old phone (they should match). Pay extra attention to your email, bank and Apple or Google account.

4. Update the phone number if it changed

If your number is new, update it on sites that use it for recovery.

5. Then wipe the old phone

Only after everything works. Keep your recovery codes handy for the first week, just in case. See Recovery codes.
