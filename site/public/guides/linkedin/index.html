Turn on two-factor for LinkedIn

Use an authenticator app like Tanooki Auth for your LinkedIn account at linkedin.com.

  1. Click your profile icon, then Settings & Privacy
  2. Under Sign-in & security, choose Two-step verification
  3. Turn it on, choose Authenticator App, and enter your password
  4. Scan the QR code with Tanooki Auth (tap +, then Scan QR)
  5. Type the 6-digit code from Tanooki Auth to finish

Good to know: LinkedIn may show a secret key instead of a QR code on some screens; Tanooki Auth can also add accounts by typing the key.

Open LinkedIn’s settings Source: LinkedIn’s help page

Checked October 30, 2026. Websites change their menus often; if a step looks different, the help page above has the latest.
